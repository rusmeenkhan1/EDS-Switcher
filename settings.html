🔄 Website Switcher Setup

Switch between your old and new website versions instantly

📖 What does this do?
This extension helps you quickly jump between two versions of the same website:
  • ✅ Your original website (the old one)
  • ✅ Your new Adobe website (the .aem.page or .aem.live version)
🎯 How to set it up:
  1. Open a page from your old website in one browser tab
  2. Open the same page from your new Adobe website in another tab
  3. Copy both URLs and paste them below
  4. Click the button - Done! The extension figures out the rest automatically ✨
💡 Example - Here's what to copy and paste:
📍 Old website URL:
https://www.mysite.com/about.html
🆕 New Adobe website URL:
https://main--mysite--company.aem.page/about
