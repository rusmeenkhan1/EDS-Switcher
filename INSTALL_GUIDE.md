# 🚀 Super Easy Installation Guide
## Website Switcher - Step by Step

> **Total time needed:** 2-3 minutes  
> **What you'll need:** Google Chrome browser  
> **Skill level:** Complete beginner - anyone can do this!

---

## 📥 Step 1: Download the Extension

1. **Find the green "Code" button** at the top of this page (on GitHub)
2. Click it and select **"Download ZIP"**
3. Your browser will download a file (usually goes to your Downloads folder)
4. **Find the downloaded ZIP file** and double-click it to extract/unzip it
5. You'll now have a folder - remember where you saved it!

---

## 🔧 Step 2: Install in Chrome

1. **Open Google Chrome**

2. **Go to the extensions page:**
   - Type `chrome://extensions/` in your address bar
   - Or click: Menu (⋮) → Extensions → Manage Extensions

3. **Turn on "Developer mode":**
   - Look for a toggle switch in the **top-right corner**
   - Click it to turn it ON (it should be blue/highlighted)

4. **Load the extension:**
   - Click the **"Load unpacked"** button (appears after turning on Developer mode)
   - A file browser window will open
   - **Find and select the folder** you extracted in Step 1
   - Click "Select" or "Open"

5. **Success!** 🎉
   - You should see the "Website Switcher" appear in your extensions list
   - The extension icon might appear in your toolbar

---

## 📌 Step 3: Pin It (Recommended)

This makes the extension easy to access:

1. Look for the **puzzle piece icon (🧩)** in your Chrome toolbar (top-right)
2. Click it to see all your extensions
3. Find **"Website Switcher"**
4. Click the **pin icon (📌)** next to it
5. The extension icon will now stay visible in your toolbar!

---

## ⚙️ Step 4: First Time Setup (This is where the magic happens!)

Now that the extension is installed, you need to connect your old and new websites:

### What You'll Need:
- One URL from your **old website** (any page)
- The **same page** from your new Adobe website

### Let's Do It:

1. **Open your old website** in one Chrome tab
   - Example: `https://www.mysite.com/about.html`

2. **Open your new Adobe website** in another tab
   - Example: `https://main--mysite--company.aem.page/about`
   - (This usually has `.aem.page` or `.aem.live` in the URL)

3. **Click the extension icon** in your Chrome toolbar

4. **Click the "⚙️ Setup Instructions" button**
   - This opens the setup page

5. **Connect your websites:**
   - **First box:** Copy and paste your **old website URL**
   - **Second box:** Copy and paste your **new Adobe website URL**
   - Click the big **"✨ Connect These Websites"** button

6. **See the success message!** 🎉
   - You'll see a green message saying your websites are connected
   - That's it - you're done!

### Adding More Websites (Optional):

If you have multiple websites to set up:
1. Check the box that says **"I want to add multiple websites one after another"**
2. Fill in the URLs for your next website pair
3. Click **"✨ Connect These Websites"** again
4. Repeat as many times as needed!

## 🎯 How to Use It Daily

Now that everything is set up, using it is super simple:

1. **Visit any page** on your old or new website
2. **Click the extension icon** in your toolbar
3. **Boom!** A new tab opens with the other version of the page

That's it! No buttons to click, no forms to fill out. Just one click and you're there! ⚡

## 🆘 Having Problems? Let's Fix Them!

### Problem: "I can't find the extension icon"
**Solution:**
1. Look for the puzzle piece icon (🧩) in your Chrome toolbar
2. Click it to see all extensions
3. Find "Website Switcher" and click the pin (📌)
4. Now it will always be visible!

### Problem: "The extension isn't in my extensions list"
**Solution:**
1. Go to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Make sure **"Developer mode"** is turned ON (top-right)
3. Try the "Load unpacked" steps again
4. Make sure you're selecting the correct folder

### Problem: "It says 'Website Not Set Up Yet'"
**Solution:**
- This means you haven't connected your websites yet
- Click the link in the message
- Follow Step 4 above to connect your websites
- Only takes 1 minute!

### Problem: "I clicked but nothing happened"
**Solution:**
1. Make sure you've set up both websites first (Step 4)
2. Make sure you're on a page from one of your connected websites
3. Try refreshing the current page
4. Close and reopen the extension popup

### Problem: "Extension stopped working"
**Solution:**
1. Go to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Find "Website Switcher"
3. Click the refresh icon (↻)
4. Try again!

## 🗑️ How to Uninstall

If you need to remove the extension:

1. Go to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Find "Website Switcher"
3. Click **"Remove"**
4. Confirm when asked

**Note:** This will delete all your saved website connections.  
**Tip:** Use "💾 Save to File" in settings first if you want to keep a backup!
